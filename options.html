Language

Stumble will mostly show sites in your language, or ones that need no reading. Sites in other languages still turn up now and then. Works best with an AI key, since the built-in list is mostly in English.

Endless stumbling

Without a key, Stumble uses its built-in list of about 180 sites. With an AI API key, it keeps finding new ones in batches of 25, shaped by what you like and skip. Usage is billed to your API account, and the key stays on this computer.

Any service that speaks the OpenAI chat format, like OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, or Ollama on your computer (http://localhost:11434/v1). Your key is sent to this address, so only use services you trust.
